## Traps this session fell into — worth not repeating

- **A passing test proved nothing three times.** `_FakeAudioSource` returned
  `Stream.empty()`, which is a recorder that died on arrival, and hid item 32
  entirely. The hazard-submit fake returned a genuine `Future<void>`, which
  cannot reproduce item 46. When a fake is simpler than the real thing in the
  dimension under test, it is not a fake, it is a different program.
- **`LocationSettings.timeLimit` is enforced platform-side.** It does nothing
  when the channel itself is unresponsive. Four separate hangs came from this;
  every `Geolocator` call needs a Dart-side `.timeout` as well.
- **Adding a parameter to `SttService.listenOnce` breaks every test fake.**
  It happened twice. Expect it.
- **The handoff before this one was wrong in three places.** Check claims
  against the tree rather than trusting the document.
