## Fixed, with tests

**The pending timer.** `CancellableDelay` (`lib/core/utils/cancellable_delay.dart`)
is a delay a `State` can take back in `dispose`. Six waits across the three
voice screens with the narrate-then-listen shape now use it — the Show Screen
overlay, the passer-by picker, the hazard hub. Cancelling is sticky, so a loop
awaiting a listen session when the widget dies cannot arm a fresh timer on its
way out.

**`cancel_route` was too greedy in both languages.** Bare `যাব না` is the last
two words of `আমি অফিসে যাব না` ("I am not going to the office"), and English
"i am not going" opens "I'm not going to lie". Both cancelled the user's real
route. Split into strong phrases (match anywhere) and weak ones (bare
negations, which count only when the phrase is essentially the whole
utterance), mirroring the emergency matcher's existing strong/weak split.

**Item 26 — onboarding losing everything.** Two faults, and the second is the
literal one: nothing recorded which step the user had reached, *and*
`chooseRole` wrote a blank `UserProfile` over the saved one with
`merge: true`. See `docs/open_bugs.md` item 26.

**Item 27 — hazard reports never saving.** The write was never attempted: an
unbounded `Geolocator.getCurrentPosition()` hung in front of it. Note for
anyone touching location anywhere in this app: **`LocationSettings.timeLimit`
is enforced by the platform plugin**, so it does nothing when the channel
itself is unresponsive. A Dart-side `.timeout` is needed as well. The same
defect was in `EmergencyService._position` and is fixed there too.

**Item 30 — hazard hub replaying the previous step.** It stopped the
recognizer on a step change but never the narrator, and `TtsService` queues.

**Item 32 — the mic not recovering after airplane mode.** The audio stream
subscription had no `onError`/`onDone`, so a recorder that died was silent —
and `isListening` (`_audioSub != null`) stayed true, which is the same item's
"mic indicator shows off when it isn't", inverted.

**Item 33 — missing onboarding synonyms.** Measured first: the reported phrase
already matched, but only by accident, on one verb shared with the label.

## Not fixed, and why

**Item 28 — caretaker communication.** The receiving half of Module 8 does not
exist. `CommunicationService`, the rules, and pairing are all correct;
`communicationsStreamProvider` is referenced only from inside
`lib/features/guardian/`, so nothing on the Disabled User's device ever listens.
Snapshot requests additionally have nothing to answer them — there is no
`camera` dependency at all, so Module 6 is unbuilt. **This is the biggest piece
of real work left.** open_bugs item 28 has the order to build it in; step 2
(memos announced aloud) needs no camera and turns "no part of it works" into a
working one-way channel.

**Item 31 — wake word with the screen off.** Manifest and wake lock are both
correct. The strongest suspect is that Android 12+ refuses a foreground-service
start from the background, and it is started on `AppLifecycleState.paused`.
That was previously unknowable because the failure was swallowed; it now names
the exception in logcat. **One device session with logcat should settle it** —
that is the cheapest next step in this file.

**Item 35 — caretaker unpair.** Blocked on a Firestore rules change: no rule
permits clearing the *other* party's `pairedUserId`. open_bugs item 35 has the
proposed rule and, importantly, why a one-sided unpair must not be shipped (it
revokes live location, alerts and communications, but leaves the caretaker able
to read the profile and change settings remotely).

**Item 34 — barge-in.** Untouched. Still a decision, not a patch.

## A note on fixtures

Two test fakes were hiding real bugs, and both are worth remembering:

- `_FakeAudioSource` returned `const Stream.empty()`, which is *done* the
  moment it is listened to — a recorder that died on arrival, not one that is
  running. It hid the whole of item 32.
- The hazard hub had no widget coverage at all, and a TTS fake that only
  recorded `speak` calls would still have shown nothing wrong for item 30: the
  hub always asked for the right text. The new fake models the real queue, so
  the test measures what reaches the speaker.
